Scope, schedule, cost, and useful terms

These key facts can help you evaluate a fence installation contractor near me:

  • Scope: The plan may cover a fence line, posts, fence sections, and gates if they’re part of the agreed work. Check that the estimate says which items are included.
  • Timing: Project timing depends on the size and layout, site access, and preparation involved. Ask for a schedule based on the actual property and the defined scope.
  • Cost factors: Materials, the amount of fencing, gates, terrain, access, and preparation may affect the overall cost. Ask the contractor to identify the factors behind the estimate instead of relying on a broad assumption.
  • Glossary: *Scope* means the work included in the agreement. A *post* supports the fence. A *fence line* describes where the fence is planned to run.
