Key facts about layout, timing and cost

A privacy fence project commonly calls for decisions about placement, height, material, gates, and the amount of fence involved. The contractor should clarify what is included, how the layout will be determined, and whether the proposal covers removal or changes to an existing fence. Do not treat these items as included unless the scope says so.

Timing varies with the size and complexity of the project, material availability, site access, and the work included. Ask for an expected schedule and how updates will be shared. Cost is also project-specific. Fence length, chosen materials, height, gate details, ground conditions, access, and any removal or repair work can affect the total scope. Request a written proposal that separates the main components.

Terms you may hear:
- Fence line: The planned path where the fence will be placed.
- Post: A vertical support that holds sections of fencing in position.
- Gate opening: The space and hardware planned for a way through the fence.
