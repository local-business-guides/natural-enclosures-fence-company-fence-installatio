Key facts about the installation process

Installation commonly involves planning the fence line, preparing the site, setting posts and fitting the selected fence sections and any planned gates. The exact steps depend on the property and the agreed scope. Discuss who is responsible for confirming the layout and how the work area will be accessed.

Keep these general project factors in mind:

  • Coverage: The length and location of the fence, plus any gates or transitions in the layout.
  • Timing: The schedule can depend on the project’s size, site preparation, access, materials and weather. Ask for a timing estimate based on your specific plan.
  • Cost: Fence length, material choice, terrain, gate details, site access and removal of an old fence can all affect the overall cost. Ask what is included and what could change the estimate.
  • Terms to know: A post supports the fence; a panel is a section fitted between supports; a gate provides an opening through the fence line.

Before choosing a contractor, compare the written scope, materials, expected schedule and any stated exclusions. Ask how the fence line will be confirmed and whether preparation or old-fence removal is part of the work. These details make it easier to understand what each proposal covers.
